What to expect

Our flyers, emails and web pages show a row of symbols so you can tell, before you arrive, what an event will be like. This page explains what each one means.

The event you scanned

Every symbol on the graphic, with a little more detail.

    How to read the symbols

    • The picture shows what it is about. Each topic always has the same picture.
    • The barsshow how much: one bar is the least, three bars is the most.
    • Tick or cross means yes or no. A tick next to quiet space means there is one.

    None of these are good or bad. Loud is not worse than quiet, only different. They are here so you can decide what works for you and plan for it.

    On everything

    Volume

    How loud will it be?

    • Quiet, level 1 of 3Quiet background music only.
    • Moderate, level 2 of 3Live, amplified music and congregational singing.
    • Loud, level 3 of 3Live, amplified music; Concert/gig style sound levels.

    People are welcome to wear earplugs/defenders at any event or service if they wish.

    Attendance

    How many people will be there?

    • 0-50, level 1 of 3Up to about 50 people.
    • 50-150, level 2 of 3Around 50 to 150 people.
    • 150+, level 3 of 3Over 150 people.

    Seating

    Where can I sit? How are the chairs set out?

    • Sit anywhere, level 1 of 3Plenty of seats, including at the back and near the door.
    • Set out in rows, level 2 of 3Seats for everyone, arranged in rows, seats near the door can be requested in advance or ask a host on the day.
    • Limited seats, level 3 of 3Most people will be standing, but some seating is available. Ask a host if you need a seat near the exit.

    Personal space

    How close will I be to other people?

    • Room around you, level 1 of 3Easy to keep space between you and others.
    • Fairly close, level 2 of 3You may be sitting next to someone you do not know.
    • Close together, level 3 of 3Shoulder to shoulder; people may move past you and have physical contact with you.

    Length

    How long will it last?

    • About 1 hour, level 1 of 3Runs straight through, no break.
    • About 1 hour 30 minutes, level 2 of 3Our standard Sunday length.
    • Over 1 hour 30 minutes, level 3 of 3Our longest gatherings. You are free to leave and come back.

    What happens

    Will I know what is coming next?

    • Set order, level 1 of 3Printed order of service; we follow it.
    • Mostly set, level 2 of 3Printed order, with room for the unplanned.
    • Led in the moment, level 3 of 3No fixed order; the shape is decided as we go.

    Quiet space

    Is there somewhere to go if I need a break?

    • AvailableA quiet room is open throughout; ask any host.
    • Not availableNo quiet room at this venue.

    Sometimes shown

    Bass

    Will you feel the low notes as well as hear them?

    • Little or none, level 1 of 3Piano, organ, voices or acoustic style instruments.
    • You will hear it, level 2 of 3A full band. Bass guitar and drums in the mix.
    • You will feel it, level 3 of 3Deep bass through the floor and in your chest. Some people love this; some find it hard.

    Lighting

    What will the lighting be like?

    • Steady, level 1 of 3Normal room lighting throughout.
    • Stage lights, level 2 of 3Lights lowered for parts of the service.
    • Moving or flashing, level 3 of 3Stage lighting that changes or flashes.

    Joining in

    Will I be asked to take part?

    • Watch only, level 1 of 3You can sit and watch the whole time.
    • Join in if you want, level 2 of 3You will be invited, never singled out.
    • Everyone takes part, level 3 of 3Standing, singing or moving is part of it.

    Still not sure about something? Ask any host on the day. They will be glad to help, and can show you to the quiet room if there is one.
